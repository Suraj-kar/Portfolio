Frequently Asked Questions

What technologies do you work with?

I work with Python, Django, SQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SCSS and collaborate with Flutter and Next JS. I also use WebSockets for real-time features and machine learning for data projects.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines depend on scope. A simple Django app may take 2–3 weeks, while a full-stack project with real-time features can take 5–6 weeks.

What's your development process?

My process follows four phases: Discovery (requirements & planning), Design (wireframes & prototypes), Development (iterative builds with regular demos) and Launch (deployment, testing & handoff).
